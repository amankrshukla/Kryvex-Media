FAQ

Common questions

Is 72 hours really enough time for a quality website?+

For a standard business website, yes — the timeline works because we run design, build and testing as one continuous process with one in-house team, not a chain of hand-offs between freelancers. Larger or highly custom projects are scoped individually, and we'll always tell you upfront if something needs more time.

What's included in the free hosting?+

Hosting for your website is included as part of your package — you don't get a separate hosting bill to manage on top of your site. We'll walk you through the specifics for your project when we scope it with you.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. There's no lock-in contract for your website build. If you choose to continue with us for ongoing marketing afterward, that's month-to-month too — you stay because it's working, not because you're tied in.

Can you build an online store, not just a business site?+

Yes — e-commerce functionality (product listings, checkout) can be built in. Larger catalogues or complex integrations may need a bit more time than the standard 72-hour build, which we'll flag upfront.

Do you work with businesses outside India?+

Yes — this is a remote-first service available worldwide. Location doesn't limit who we can build a website for.

What happens after my site launches?+

You own your site. If you want ongoing support, updates, or marketing help (SEO, ads, content), that's available month-to-month — never required.
