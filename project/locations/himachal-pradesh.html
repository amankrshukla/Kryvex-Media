What Powers The State

The five pillars of Himachal's rise

From apple orchards to pharma plants — the foundations shaping Himachal's growth story.

01Apple Orchards
🍎Apple Orchards
→ Fruit Bowl Of India

Shimla and Kullu's apple orchards make Himachal India's leading apple-producing state, anchoring a huge share of the rural economy.

02Hill Tourism
🏔️Hill Tourism
→ Mountain Economy

Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala draw millions of domestic and international tourists each year, powering the state's largest industry.

03Hydropower
💧Hydropower
→ Clean Energy Hub

Himachal's rivers and dams make it one of India's largest hydroelectric power producers, feeding the national grid.

04Pharma Belt
💊Pharma Belt
→ Manufacturing Hub

The Baddi-Barotiwala-Nalagarh industrial belt near Solan makes Himachal one of India's largest pharmaceutical manufacturing hubs.

05Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj
🕉️Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj
→ Spiritual Tourism

Home to the Dalai Lama and a large Tibetan community, Dharamshala draws spiritual and wellness travellers from across the world.

Business Growth & Marketing

The next booking starts on a phone screen.

As Himachal's travellers, orchardists and traders search, book, and pay from their phones, the businesses that show up online are the ones that grow. Discovery now starts on Google and Instagram, trust is built through reviews and content, and a single well-run campaign can reach an entire valley.
