What Powers The State

The five pillars of Telangana's rise

From ancient temples to tech towers — the foundations shaping Telangana's growth story.

01HITEC City
🏙️HITEC City
→ IT & Startups

Hyderabad's HITEC City and Cyberabad host global tech giants and a thriving startup scene, anchoring Telangana's digital economy.

02Genome Valley
💊Genome Valley
→ Pharma Capital

Hyderabad is India's bulk-drug capital, home to some of the world's largest vaccine and pharmaceutical manufacturers.

03Kakatiya Heritage
🏛️Kakatiya Heritage
→ Temple Tourism

Warangal's Thousand Pillar Temple and the UNESCO-listed Ramappa Temple draw history and heritage tourists year-round.

04Turmeric Belt
🟡Turmeric Belt
→ Agricultural Trade

Nizamabad's turmeric markets and Telangana's rice and cotton belts anchor a large share of the state's rural economy.

05Singareni Coalfields
⛏️Singareni Coalfields
→ Mining & Industry

The Singareni collieries around Khammam and Kothagudem make Telangana a major coal-mining and industrial state.
