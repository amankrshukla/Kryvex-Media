What Powers The State

The five pillars of West Bengal's rise

From a Salt Lake IT corridor to the world's largest mangrove forest — the foundations shaping West Bengal's growth story.

01Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata
💻Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata
→ East India's IT & BPO Hub

Kolkata's dedicated IT corridor anchors eastern India's largest technology and business process economy.

02Howrah-Durgapur Industrial Belt
🏭Howrah-Durgapur Industrial Belt
→ Steel, Engineering & Jute

Decades of steel plants, engineering works and jute mills anchor West Bengal's industrial base.

03Darjeeling & Dooars Tea Gardens
🍃Darjeeling & Dooars Tea Gardens
→ World-Famous Tea & Hill Tourism

The Darjeeling and Dooars tea gardens supply some of the world's most prized tea, alongside a thriving hill tourism economy.

04Sundarbans
🐅Sundarbans
→ World's Largest Mangrove Forest

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to the Royal Bengal Tiger, driving eco-tourism across the delta.

05Haldia & Kolkata Port
🚢Haldia & Kolkata Port
→ Major Port & Petrochemical Hub

The Hooghly river ports at Kolkata and Haldia anchor eastern India's shipping, petrochemical and trade economy.
