What Powers The State

The five pillars of Chhattisgarh's rise

From steel plants to mineral belts — the foundations shaping Chhattisgarh's growth story.

01Steel & Industry
🏭Steel & Industry
→ Industrial Powerhouse

Bhilai, Raipur and Durg form one of India's largest steel and industrial manufacturing corridors.

02Coal & Power
⚡Coal & Power
→ Energy Hub

Korba and the state's coalfields make Chhattisgarh one of India's top power-generating states.

03Mineral Wealth
⛏️Mineral Wealth
→ Mining Belt

Rich deposits of iron ore, coal and bauxite anchor a huge share of the state's economy.

04Agriculture
🌾Agriculture
→ Rice Bowl Of India

Chhattisgarh's fertile plains make it one of India's leading rice-producing states.

05Bastar & Tribal Belt
🌳Bastar & Tribal Belt
→ Forest Economy

Bastar's forests, waterfalls and tribal handicrafts are drawing a fast-growing tourism trade.
