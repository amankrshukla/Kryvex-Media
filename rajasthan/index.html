The five pillars of Rajasthan's rise

From a Pink City startup corridor to the marble capital of India — the foundations shaping Rajasthan's growth story.

01Jaipur Startup Corridor
🏙️Jaipur Startup Corridor

02Jodhpur Handicraft Cluster
🪵Jodhpur Handicraft Cluster
→ India's Handicraft Export Capital

03Makrana & Kishangarh Marble Belt
🪨Makrana & Kishangarh Marble Belt
→ Marble Capital of India

04Kota Coaching Economy
📚Kota Coaching Economy
→ India's Coaching Capital

Hundreds of thousands of students move to Kota each year for JEE and NEET coaching, powering a booming ed-tech and hospitality economy.

🏜️Udaipur & Jaisalmer Tourism
→ Global Tourism & Wedding Destination

From Udaipur's lake palaces to the Thar desert dunes of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan is India's top international tourism draw.
