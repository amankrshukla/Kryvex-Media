Common questions

What does a digital marketing agency do for a small business?+

A full-service digital marketing agency runs SEO, paid ads, social media, content, and web design as one connected system — rather than as disconnected freelance tasks — so every channel feeds the same growth goal: more qualified leads at a lower cost per acquisition.

How much does digital marketing cost for a small business?+

Pricing depends on your goals and the channels you need, so we don't publish one-size-fits-all packages. Book a free 30-minute call and we'll give you a transparent quote after a quick audit — no obligation and no long-term contracts.

How fast can Kryvex Media get started?+

Fast — that's the whole point. You'll have a free audit within 48 hours of your call, a strategy you can approve in days, and most clients see their first campaigns go live inside the first two weeks. No drawn-out onboarding, no waiting a week just to get a reply.

How is Kryvex Media different from other agencies?+

Two things: speed and integration. Most agencies take a week to reply and specialise in a single channel, handing the rest to freelancers. Kryvex sends your audit in 48 hours, gets campaigns live in days, and runs SEO, PPC, social, web and content under one team and one strategy — so nothing stalls waiting on a hand-off.

How long until I see results?+

It depends on the channel. Paid ads can start driving enquiries within a few weeks, while SEO typically builds over three to six months. We'll map out a realistic timeline for your goals in your free audit — we don't promise guaranteed rankings, only honest expectations and fast execution.

Do you require long-term contracts?+

No. Kryvex Media works without long-term contracts and gives you a transparent quote after your free 30-minute strategy call. You stay because the results are working, not because a contract locks you in.
