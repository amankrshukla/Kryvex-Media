What Powers The State

The five pillars of Gujarat's rise

From the world's diamond capital to India's first international financial centre — the foundations shaping Gujarat's growth story.

01Surat Diamond Bourse
💎Surat Diamond Bourse
→ World's Diamond Capital

Surat cuts and polishes the vast majority of the world's diamonds, anchoring a massive export economy.

02GIFT City, Gandhinagar
🏦GIFT City, Gandhinagar
→ India's International Financial Hub

India's first international financial services centre, drawing global banks and fintech firms.

03Jamnagar Refinery Complex
🛢️Jamnagar Refinery Complex
→ World's Largest Oil Refinery

Jamnagar hosts the world's largest single-site oil refining complex, anchoring Gujarat's energy sector.

04Ahmedabad Textile & Startup Corridor
🧵Ahmedabad Textile & Startup Corridor
→ Manchester of India

From the white salt desert of the Rann of Kutch to the Somnath temple and Kandla and Mundra ports, Gujarat's coastline drives tourism and trade alike.
