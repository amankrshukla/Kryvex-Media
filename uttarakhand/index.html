The five pillars of Uttarakhand's rise

From sacred rivers to summit trails — the foundations shaping Uttarakhand's growth story.

01Char Dham
🛕Char Dham
→ Spiritual Economy

Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri and Yamunotri draw millions of pilgrims each year, powering the state's largest and oldest industry.

02Ganga
🌊Ganga
→ Yoga & Wellness

Rishikesh and Haridwar sit on the Ganga's banks, anchoring a booming wellness, adventure-sport and retreat-tourism economy.

03SIDCUL
🏭SIDCUL
→ Industrial Belt

Pharma, FMCG and auto-component manufacturers across Haridwar and Pantnagar make Uttarakhand one of India's fastest-growing industrial states.
