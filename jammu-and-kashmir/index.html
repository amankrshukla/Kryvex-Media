What Powers The State

The five pillars of Jammu & Kashmir's rise

From saffron to skiing — the foundations shaping J&K's growth story.

01Horticulture
🍎Horticulture
→ Apple Economy

Kashmir grows the bulk of India's apples, alongside walnuts and cherries — a huge rural economy.

02Tourism
🏔️Tourism
→ Paradise On Earth

Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg make tourism a cornerstone of the economy.

03Saffron
🌸Saffron
→ World-Famous Spice

Pampore's saffron is among the world's finest, a high-value signature of the Kashmir valley.

04Handicraft
🧶Handicraft
→ Craft Economy

Pashmina, carpets, papier-mâché and walnut wood carving are prized and exported worldwide.

05Pilgrimage
🕉️Pilgrimage
→ Faith Economy

Vaishno Devi and Amarnath draw tens of millions of pilgrims, powering a large services economy.
