Why these three numbers: Starter sits just above a realistic 15-hour floor. Growth is roughly 2× Starter but delivers more than 2× the value, which is what makes it the rational pick. Full is priced so that if a client takes it, you can afford to give it the attention it needs. Move all three together as your capacity and confidence grow.

Minimum engagement3 months. Under that, SEO can't show anything and you'll be blamed for it.

First client / case-study dealUp to 25% off in exchange for a written testimonial and permission to publish results. Put it in writing.
Payment termsRetainers: in advance, on the 1st. Projects: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.

5. What you cannot promise

You have no client results yet, so every claim you make has to be about process, not outcomes. This protects you legally and reputationally.

Don't say
  • "We'll get you to #1 on Google"
  • "We average 4.2x ROAS"
  • "Guaranteed leads in 30 days"
  • "We've grown 30+ businesses"
Say instead
  • "Here's exactly what we'll do in the first 90 days"
  • "Every rupee tracked to a real lead — you'll see it monthly"
  • "Ads can bring enquiries in weeks; SEO compounds over months"
  • "You'd be an early client, which is why the pricing reflects that"
Being new is not a weakness — hiding it is. "You'd be one of our first clients, so you get more of my attention than you would at a big agency, and the price reflects that" is a genuinely strong pitch. It's also true.
